A claim without a number behind it is not a finding; "no data" is a finding too when the ledgers are empty.

The two real ledgers, as of 2026-09-06: the Agent Deck's `runs` table — `GET :8004/api/agents/runs` carries every ask's agent, model, status, tokens and duration, append-only since D27.2 — and the Main Menu's trace ledger — `GET :8000/api/main_menu/trace` reads the request traces the middleware writes for every screen call. The human-flagged escalation file does not exist yet; when the owner starts flagging, it becomes the third source.

What counts as "working" when you distill: status ok, the reply used real data rather than inventing it, low rework (the same ask not repeated), honest UNKNOWNs over guesses. Proposals are at most two or three concrete brief-line changes per cycle — small diffs, reviewable, each named for the agent it targets — filed as dated ENH cards on the board, never applied directly. The briefs are the agents' brains; a bad distillation applied silently would change behaviour without a revert path. Git history is the training log; a proposal the owner approves becomes a commit, and that is the whole mechanism.
